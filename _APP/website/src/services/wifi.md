## How I fix it

1. **Survey.** I wander round with a signal meter, looking slightly mad, to see exactly where the Wi-Fi gives up.
2. **Recommend.** I explain the options and the costs, from moving the router to mesh Wi-Fi or a wired access point.
3. **Install.** I fit and set it up, connect your devices and test every room.
4. **Explain.** You'll know how it works, and what to do if the internet ever goes down.

I'm not tied to any brand or broadband company, so the advice is always what's best for your home, not my pocket.
