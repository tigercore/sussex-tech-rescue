## Do these things now

1. **Hang up and disconnect.** If someone is on your computer, end the call, then turn off the Wi-Fi or unplug the internet cable.
2. **Call your bank.** Dial **159** from any phone to reach most UK banks' fraud teams, or use the number on the back of your card. Use a different phone if you can.
3. **Change your passwords.** Start with your email, then online banking, from a device you know is safe.
4. **Report it.** Report fraud to Action Fraud, the UK's national reporting service. Forward scam texts to **7726** and scam emails to **report@phishing.gov.uk**.
5. **Call me.** I'll check your computer or phone, remove anything the scammer installed and make sure your accounts are secure.

</div>

## How I help after a scam

- Find and boot out remote-access software or anything else the scammer installed
- Check your email for sneaky rules or forwarding the scammer may have set up
- Lock down your accounts with new passwords and two-step sign-in
- Explain what happened, without judgement, so you'll spot it next time
- Talk it through with family members too, if that helps
