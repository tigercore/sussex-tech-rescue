## Getting the basics right

Here's a secret: most smart home problems are really Wi-Fi problems in disguise. Before fitting anything, I check your signal reaches where the gadget's going, and fix it if not. Fewer drop-outs, fewer shouting-at-Alexa moments.

I can also help you choose the right kit before you buy, so you don't end up with three apps that refuse to talk to each other. And it's not all gimmicks: smart speakers, video calling and reminders can make a real difference for older relatives living on their own.
