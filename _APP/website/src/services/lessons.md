## How lessons work

1. **Tell me what you want to learn.** Big or small, from "how do I send a photo" to "teach me Excel".
2. **I come to you.** Lessons happen on your own device, at your own table, at your own pace.
3. **You do the clicking.** I explain, you try it. That's how it sticks.
4. **Notes to keep.** I leave simple step-by-step notes for later.

Lessons are charged at my normal rate, per 30 minutes. Want a few sessions to build up your confidence? Just say, and we'll book them in.
